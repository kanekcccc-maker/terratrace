SDG 15 Impact

Protecting heritage also protects living landscapes.

Archaeological sites are not isolated objects. They are part of soils, habitats, water systems, cultural routes and long-lived relationships between people and land.

One landscape, many values

Sustainable land use must account for what is visible—and what is buried.

By identifying risk before construction, TerraTrace supports conservation choices that can reduce habitat loss, soil disturbance and destruction of cultural evidence at the same time.
