The Problem

Construction is silently destroying archaeological heritage.

Many archaeological sites remain undiscovered beneath forests, fields and deserts. Once heavy excavation begins, historical layers can disappear in hours.

Invisible until disturbed

The absence of a marker does not mean the absence of history.

Archaeological evidence often survives as delicate soil layers, foundations, burial structures, artefact concentrations and ancient landscape patterns. Standard construction surveys may overlook these traces until machinery has already removed them.

The planning gap

Traditional protection often begins after a site is found.

TerraTrace shifts the timeline. Instead of waiting for accidental discovery, it identifies patterns of construction pressure and directs attention to vulnerable land before irreversible damage occurs.
