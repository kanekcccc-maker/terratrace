A shared landscape

History and nature exist on the same ground.

TerraTrace reframes archaeological protection as part of sustainable land management. It helps planners see what lies beneath a site before irreversible construction begins.

01 · Detect

See construction pressure early.

Satellite imagery highlights new roads, excavation, land clearing and rapidly expanding developments.

02 · Compare

Connect risk with heritage data.

GIS layers compare activity with known sites, protected landscapes and high-potential archaeological zones.

03 · Act

Turn evidence into timely alerts.

Researchers and authorities receive clear, location-based warnings before damage becomes permanent.

Technology can help development move forward without erasing what came before.
