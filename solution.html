How TerraTrace works.

A repeatable monitoring workflow transforms remote sensing data into clear, location-based archaeological risk alerts.

Monitoring workflow

Five steps from observation to action.

The system combines automated detection with expert review. It is designed to support—not replace—archaeologists, conservation specialists and planning authorities.

01

Satellite monitoring

Frequent imagery creates a visual record of land-use change across selected regions.

02

AI detects new construction

Computer vision flags excavation, roads, cleared vegetation, building footprints and industrial expansion.

03

GIS compares heritage zones

Detected change is compared with protected sites, historic maps, survey records and predictive archaeological layers.

04

Risk assessment

Each location receives a transparent score based on proximity, construction intensity, landscape sensitivity and confidence.

05

Automatic alerts

Researchers and authorities receive a map, imagery comparison and recommended next action.
