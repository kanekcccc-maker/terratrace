Real Cases

Development pressure is reshaping heritage landscapes worldwide.

These cases show how roads, dams, urban expansion, tourism and industrial projects can place archaeological landscapes under pressure.

United KingdomHigh scrutiny

Stonehenge

Road and tunnel proposals have raised concerns about construction impacts across the wider archaeological landscape surrounding the monument.

TurkeySubmerged

Hasankeyf

Much of the ancient settlement and its surrounding landscape was flooded following dam construction, despite extensive documentation and rescue efforts.

EgyptOngoing pressure

Ancient Egyptian sites

Fast urban growth, transport infrastructure and agricultural expansion can place undocumented sites and historic landscape settings at risk.

ItalyManaged risk

Pompeii

The site faces the combined pressure of mass tourism, conservation demands and development in the surrounding urban landscape.

Central AsiaFragmented

Silk Road heritage

Industrial projects, mining, road construction and new infrastructure can fragment routes and damage archaeological remains across vast landscapes.
